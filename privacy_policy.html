Ludo SudoX

Privacy Policy

Your game data stays on your device. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob.

Last updated: 8 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Ludo SudoX ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information. The App is published by Kesar Apps.

By installing or using Ludo SudoX, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

In short: Ludo SudoX has no accounts and no servers of our own, and everything the game itself saves stays on your device. The one exception is advertising: the App shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and Google's ads SDK collects limited data (such as your advertising ID) to serve and measure them — see Section 5.

1.Information We Collect

We do not run any servers of our own and do not collect personal data about you directly. Ludo SudoX has no backend, no accounts, and no sign-in. The only data that leaves your device is collected by Google AdMob, the service that shows rewarded ads — described in Section 5.

The App's own code does not access, collect, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Your precise location
  • Your photos, camera, or microphone
  • Any usage analytics or behavioural data of our own

The Google AdMob SDK does, however, collect certain identifiers and ad-interaction data — including your advertising ID — as described in Section 5.

2.Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To make the game work, Ludo SudoX saves certain information only on your device, using your device's local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. It includes:

  • Game progress and saved matches
  • In-game coin balance, daily-login streak, and power-up usage counts
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, notification, and language preferences)
  • Play statistics (e.g. play time, win record) shown on the in-app Stats screen
  • Onboarding completion status

This information is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App (see Section 6).

3.Device Permissions

Ludo SudoX requests a small number of Android permissions, used only for the features described below:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show local reminders you can enable or disable, such as your daily-reward streak nudge. All reminders are scheduled and shown by your own device. No notification is sent from any server.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to re-schedule those local reminders after your device restarts, so an enabled reminder is not lost on reboot.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) — to provide haptic feedback during gameplay.

You can disable notifications at any time from the App's Settings or your device settings.

4.Bug Reports (Optional, You Initiate)

Ludo SudoX includes an optional "report a bug" feature. It only does anything if you choose to use it. If you tap to send a bug report, the App prepares a diagnostic package and opens your device's standard share menu, where you decide whether and where to send it (for example, by emailing it to us for support). The App itself does not send your bug report anywhere automatically.

If you choose to send a bug report, the package you share may include:

  • A screenshot of the current screen
  • Your device model and operating-system version, and the App version
  • The current game state and your App settings
  • A short history of your recent in-app actions and recent error messages, to help us diagnose the issue

This information is used only to investigate the problem you report. It is shared only if and where you choose to send it, and it does not include any advertising identifier or persistent device identifier. If you do not use the bug-report feature, none of this leaves your device.

5.Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ludo SudoX displays rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You choose when to watch an ad (to earn in-game coins or an extra power-up use); ads are never forced and the game is fully playable without watching any.

To serve and measure these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares certain data with Google and its advertising partners, including your device's advertising ID, App Set ID, IP address, diagnostic information, and ad-interaction data. This is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are served.

Aside from Google AdMob, Ludo SudoX includes no other third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services, and we do not sell your data.

6.Data Retention and Deletion

Because all App data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control of it. To delete it, you can:

  • Clear the App's storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Ludo SudoX → Storage → Clear data), or
  • Uninstall the App.

Either action permanently removes all locally stored game and settings data. We retain nothing, because we never receive anything.

7.Children's Privacy

Ludo SudoX is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App shows ads through Google AdMob, it is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us using the details in Section 12.

8.Security

Because the game stores its data only on your device and sends us none of it, your game data is protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock and operating-system protections). We do not hold any of your data on servers that could be breached. (Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob, as described in Section 5.)

9.Your Privacy Rights

Privacy laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) give you rights over personal data held about you. We ourselves hold no personal data about you — the game stores everything on your device, which you can clear at any time (see Section 6). Data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google; you can exercise your choices over it through your device's ad settings (Section 5) and Google's own privacy controls. If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

10.International Users

The game processes its own data only on your device, wherever you are located. We ourselves receive no data, so we transfer none across borders. Data collected by Google AdMob (see Section 5) may be processed by Google on servers in other countries, in line with Google's Privacy Policy.

11.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and publish the new policy at this same address. Material changes will be reflected before the relevant feature is released. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
